A valley is a low area of land between hills or mountains, often with a river running through it.

A waterfall is a natural feature where water flows over a vertical drop or cliff, creating a stunning visual display.

Wildlife

Wildlife refers to undomesticated animals and plants that live in their natural habitats, contributing to biodiversity.

Rope Bridge

Mountain peak

A mountain peak is a large landform that rises prominently above its surroundings, typically having a peak or summit.

Climbing

Climbing is an activity that involves ascending mountains, cliffs, or other vertical surfaces using physical strength and skill.

Snow

Snow is frozen precipitation that falls from the atmosphere in the form of ice crystals, often covering mountainous regions.
